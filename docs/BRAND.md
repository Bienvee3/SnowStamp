# SnowStamp - Brand

## Name
**SnowStamp**. Write it as one word, camel-cased `SnowStamp` in text, `snowstamp` in handles and domains.

## Positioning
- ES (primary): *Cada estación es un sello. Tu pasaporte de esquí y snowboard.*
- EN: *Every resort is a stamp. Your ski and snowboard passport.*
- Fixed subtitle on every asset: **"Tu pasaporte de esquí y snowboard."** It prevents "Snow..." from being read as a snow report app.
- Product promise: light up the resorts you have been to, see your % per country, continent and world, compare with friends.
- Audience: people who go to ski resorts, whether they ski or snowboard. Primary market: Spanish-speaking.

## Visual identity
- Logo: rounded-square icon, blue (`#2699DB`) with a white perforated postage stamp holding a snowflake (`docs/assets/logo.png`). Wordmark in Arial Black (video); no wordmark in the icon.
- Palette (from the logo): logo blue `#2699DB`, white, deep blue `#0C467A` (text, pins), light blue tints for visited countries, cool grey for unvisited land. Orange `#FF843C` is reserved for a friend's data in comparisons.
- Map style is the brand: fix background, visited colour, unvisited colour and percentage typography, and reuse them in every piece.

## Content pillars (first batch)
1. Map of Spain with resorts lighting up one by one.
2. "I have X% of the world" with a sample map.
3. Challenge a friend: who has skied more resorts?
4. Before/after: messy spreadsheet vs. the map.
5. Direct question to the audience: how many resorts have you been to?

Rules: always state that SnowStamp is in development with a waitlist. Never present it as an available app.

## Naming history
Candidates evaluated before choosing SnowStamp, and why they lost:
| Name | Reason |
|---|---|
| ski-been | Derived from the existing "Been" brand; weak for trademark and search. |
| SnowNow | Reads as a snow-report app; `snownow.com` is for sale at a premium. |
| WhereIsNow / WhereISnow | Lowercase collapses to `whereisnow`, read as "where is now". |
| WhereISki / WhereISkied / whereskied | Exclude snowboarders; `WhereISki` close to the iSKI brand; `whereskied` loses the subject "I". |

## Naming risks
- `snowstamp.com` belongs to an unrelated Greek T-shirt printing business. Use `.app`/`.es`.
- "Snow Stamp" is also the name of a Discord snowflake-to-timestamp tool (GitHub); expect search-result noise.
- Not checked: trademark registries (TMview, OEPM), app stores, other social platforms. DNS showing no records for a domain does not prove it is unregistered.

## Privacy and data
- Waitlist collects email and survey answers in Google Forms/Sheets, with explicit consent and a deletion contact. Not legal advice.
- When resort data comes from OpenStreetMap/OpenSkiMap, show the ODbL attribution.
